The short version

  • We never ask for your name, email address, phone number or contacts.
  • Your progress is stored against a randomly generated anonymous ID.
  • Ads and analytics only run if you agree to them, and you can change your mind.
  • You can download or permanently delete everything from inside the game.

What the game stores

Only if you agree: ads

The game shows optional rewarded video ads through Google AdMob — you only ever see one by choosing to watch it in exchange for continuing a run. If you consent, AdMob may use your device's advertising identifier to select ads and to measure whether they worked.

In the EU, the UK and Switzerland the choice is presented through Google's certified consent management platform. On iOS, Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt appears separately and is a genuine choice: declining it does not change the game, and you will still see ads, just less relevant ones. You can reopen these choices at any time from Settings → Privacy & Data in the game, or in your device's own privacy settings.

Google's handling of this data is described at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Only if you agree: analytics

With your consent, the game reports anonymous, aggregated events through Firebase Analytics — that a run started, which level it ended on, whether an ad was completed, and when the game had to lower its graphics quality to keep a steady frame rate. These tell us which levels are too hard and which devices struggle. They are tied to the same anonymous ID as your progress and contain nothing that identifies you personally.

Crash reports

When the game hits an unexpected error it may send the error message, a stack trace, the anonymous ID and what the game was doing at the time to our error-reporting service so we can fix it. Nothing else about you is included, and web addresses are stripped before sending.

Who else sees it

We do not sell your data and we do not share it for advertising beyond what is described above. Three processors handle it on our behalf: Google Firebase (authentication, database, hosting, analytics), Google AdMob (ads), and our error-reporting provider. Leaderboards are public by design: your anonymous display name, score, level and country are visible to other players. Nothing else is.

How long we keep it

Your profile is kept while the game is installed and for as long as the account exists. Run tickets and short-lived anti-cheat records expire automatically within days. Deleting your account removes the rest, as below.

Your choices

  • Download your data. Settings → Privacy & Data → Download My Data gives you a JSON file of everything held against your ID.
  • Delete everything. Settings → Privacy & Data → Delete My Account permanently removes your profile, runs and leaderboard entries. This cannot be undone.
  • Change your mind about ads or analytics at any time from the same screen.

If you are in the EU, the UK, California or another region with equivalent law, these are the same rights you have to access, correct, port and erase your data. Because the account is anonymous, the in-game buttons are the fastest way to exercise them — we have no other way to find your record. You may also contact us at [CONTACT EMAIL], or complain to your local data protection authority.

Children

Breaking Bad Brick is not directed at children and we do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 13 (or the equivalent age in your country). If you believe a child has given us data, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes

If this policy changes materially we will update the date at the top and show a notice in the game before the change takes effect.
